## 🚀 Who we are

We build the quiet plumbing behind good trading tools — the part that turns
messy market feeds into data you can actually reason about.

No scraping to maintain. No sessions to babysit. No midnight breakage when a
broker changes a shape. Just clean, steady market data, delivered to whatever
you're building.

<div align="center">

> **Clean data in. Confident decisions out.** ✨

## 🧩 What we deliver

|:--:|:--|:--|
| 🕯️ | **Candles & price action** | Structured candles across multiple timeframes, ready to chart. |
| 💹 | **Live prices** | Current market pricing that keeps your interface honest. |

## 💎 Why developers choose us

- 🔑 **Private access** — your own credentials, not a shared feed everyone hammers at once.
- 🪶 **Lightweight by design** — plain, predictable data. Nothing bloated to parse, nothing to unwrap.
- 🛡️ **Read-only, always** — we serve information. We never touch accounts, orders or positions.
- ⚡ **Fast to integrate** — if you can make an HTTP request, you can ship with us today.
- 🧪 **Stable shapes** — responses stay consistent, so your code stops breaking on Tuesdays.
- 📚 **Documented properly** — every product explained on the site, no archaeology required.
